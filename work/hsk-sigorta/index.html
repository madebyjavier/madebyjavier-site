Insurance tends to sound cold. The goal for this insurance agency's Instagram is a feed that feels warm and funny without losing trust.

The work

The task

Ongoing art direction for the Instagram of HSK Sigorta, an insurance agency based in Turkey. Make insurance feel warm and funny, and keep it trustworthy.

What I do
  • Built a feed out of formats, so every post has a job and the grid never repeats itself: absurd scenarios, product posts, a myths series, a brand carousel, weekly stories with a recurring Siamese cat mascot, and commemorative posts.
  • Direct the imagery for each concept. The images are AI-generated under my direction, then graded in Lightroom and built into the layout.
  • Kept the brand tight. Navy, red and white come from the logo, with heavy type and short headlines.
Credits

Art direction, feed visual system and post design by me. Copy by the agency's copywriting team.
