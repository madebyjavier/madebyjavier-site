Kids' Boots.

A short animated social spot for a children's rain boot with a lion print.

Role
Concept, animation & compositing
Scope
Animated social spot, part of a series built on the same idea
Tools
After Effects
Format
Vertical, for social media

The work

The task

DZN Studios supplied the raw elements: the illustration, the product photo, the logo, the music and the sound effects. The job was to turn them into a short social spot for a lion-print rain boot.

What I did
  • Came up with the concept. An illustrated lion sits on the savanna, takes a breath and roars. The roar reveals the real boots in the same landscape.
  • Built the sequence and animated it. The product blurs out, the lion returns and the word "Lion" builds in letter by letter.
  • Made two worlds feel like one. The hard part was making an illustration and a real photo share a scene, so I kept a single background throughout and none of the changes reads as a cut.
One background, no cuts. An illustration and a real product photo, held in a single scene.
