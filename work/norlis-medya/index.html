Norlis Medya.

A 30-second vertical video for Norlis Medya, a social media agency in Turkey, to introduce its services to new clients.

Role
Visual direction, screen design, motion design & animation
Scope
30-second agency promo
Tools
After Effects
Format
Vertical, for social media

The work

The task

Introduce the agency and its services to new clients in 30 seconds. Norlis Medya supplied the script, the logo and the music. The piece rests on one idea: being on social media isn't enough.

What I did
  • Opened on the idea. A line falls apart and is replaced by three words that sum up how the agency works: visible, felt and results-driven.
  • Built the story around it. The brand is introduced, its services are listed and a client satisfaction meter runs from 0 to 100, before closing on the core message: "We don't sell reach. We deliver growth."
  • Designed everything else on screen. The purple gradient, the type, every layout, the satisfaction meter and the contact card.
  • Cut it all to the music. Lines blur and disperse to hand off to the next, the logo reveals with a glow, and a lavender light sweep shifts the mood right where the services begin.
We don't sell reach. We deliver growth. Script, logo and music in; everything on screen designed and animated.
