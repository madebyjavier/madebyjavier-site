The work

The task

Yapı Kredi Play is a youth card its audience saw only as “the card with no fees.” For the competition, we set out to reposition it as a lifestyle companion for university students aged 18 to 26.

The insight

Young people fear that money will let them down at the worst moment. “Başınıza bu da gelecek” is a Turkish phrase that warns of trouble ahead, roughly “this is coming your way.” The campaign flips it: what comes your way with Play is discounts, points and freedom.

What I did
  • Turned the phrase into a gesture. Hands on the head, the pose of worry, becomes a pose of surprise. A triangle that echoes a play button frames every student, and the rewards spill out of the card above them.
  • Built the system. A blue and orange key visual, type and colour, and an icon set for the rewards: tech, cinema, clothes, food and payments.
  • Took it everywhere students are. Metro, billboards, bus shelters, overpasses and a festival stage.
  • Carried the line to the card itself. The delivery envelope reads “Başınıza gelecekler bu zarfın içinde!”, what's coming your way is inside this envelope, framed by the same triangle.
  • Extended it to social for LinkedIn, X, TikTok and Instagram.
My role

Lead designer in a nine-person team. I worked on every design deliverable and had the final say on visual decisions.
